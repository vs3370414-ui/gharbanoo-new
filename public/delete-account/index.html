अकाउंट कैसे डिलीट करें

GharBanoo ऐप में साइन-इन करें, फिर Profile (घर मालिक) या Dashboard (एक्सपर्ट/दुकान) खोलें, नीचे स्क्रॉल करें और Delete account दबाएं। दोनों पुष्टि (confirmation) पूरी करें। ऐप नहीं खुल रहा? अपने अकाउंट वाले ईमेल से gharbanoo@gmail.com पर लिखें।

Delete in the app

  1. Open the GharBanoo app and sign in with the Google account you used before.
  2. Go to the right screen for your account:
    • Homeowner: the Profile tab.
    • Service provider: your Dashboard.
    • Shop owner: your Shop dashboard.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  4. Read the message, tap Continue, then tap Delete permanently.

Your account is deleted straight away. This cannot be undone.

Can't use the app?

Email gharbanoo@gmail.com from the email address of your GharBanoo account, with the subject "Account deletion request". We may ask you to confirm the request, so that no one else can delete your account. We complete email requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account and account record
  • Your notifications, likes, reviews and content reports
  • Your private invoices
  • Profile, cover, portfolio and shop images stored with us
  • For providers and shop owners: your public listing and its reviews and likes

What may remain

We keep a small record that the deletion was completed, so that your old sign-in can't recreate data. We may keep limited information when the law requires it or for security reasons. Invoice PDFs already saved on a phone or shared through WhatsApp or other apps cannot be removed by us.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
