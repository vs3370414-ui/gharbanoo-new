Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

GHARBANOO TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED, India, operates GharBanoo. Questions can be sent to gharbanoo@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Sign-in information: Google display name, email address, profile photo, and Firebase user ID when you use Google Sign-In.
  • Account and marketplace information: selected role, provider or shop profile details, ratings, reviews, likes, and other content you submit.
  • Images: profile, cover, portfolio, and supported shop images you upload.
  • Invoice information: customer name and contact details, items or services, rates, notes, discounts, totals, and invoice metadata entered by provider or shop owners.
  • Technical information: limited app, device, usage, and crash information through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. We do not intentionally send review text, email addresses, phone numbers, names, or exact addresses to Analytics.

How we use information

We use information for authentication and account management; provider and shop onboarding; public profiles, likes, ratings, and reviews; user-uploaded media; private invoice history and PDF features; service improvement; and technical crash diagnosis.

Public and private information

Approved provider and shop profiles, approved reviews, ratings, and approved public images may be visible publicly. Pending or unapproved profiles and reviews are not publicly exposed. Invoices, customer invoice details, and private account records are not publicly visible and are available only to the relevant provider or shop owner through authenticated access controls.

Third-party services and sharing

We use Google Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Google Play Services where applicable. Google and Firebase may process information as infrastructure providers. We do not sell personal data. Sharing a generated invoice PDF through WhatsApp or another app is initiated and controlled by the user; that receiving service's privacy practices apply.

Security and retention

We use Firebase security rules and authenticated access controls for profiles, uploads, invoices, and other private records. No electronic system is completely risk-free. We retain information while needed to provide GharBanoo and where required for legal, security, or operational purposes. Private invoices remain in the owner's account unless deleted through account deletion or a future product deletion feature.

Account and data deletion

You can permanently delete your account in the signed-in GharBanoo app. For help or an external deletion request, visit gharbanoo.com/delete-account or email gharbanoo@gmail.com.

Deletion removes the Firebase account, account record, user-owned likes and reviews, private invoices, and applicable Firebase-hosted profile and portfolio media. Provider and shop deletion also removes that owner's marketplace listing and its associated reviews and likes. Locally saved or previously shared invoice PDFs cannot be remotely removed by GharBanoo. Limited information may be retained where required for legal or security reasons.

Your rights, children, and updates

Where applicable, you may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. GharBanoo is not intended for children under 13. We may update this policy as our services or legal obligations change and will post the updated version here.
