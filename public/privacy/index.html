Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

GHARBANOO TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED, India ("GharBanoo", "we") operates the GharBanoo Android app and this website. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions: gharbanoo@gmail.com.

सारांश (हिंदी में)

हम सिर्फ़ वही जानकारी लेते हैं जो ऐप चलाने के लिए ज़रूरी है: Google साइन-इन की जानकारी, आपकी प्रोफ़ाइल, रिव्यू और फोटो। ऐप में कोई विज्ञापन नहीं है, हम विज्ञापन ID (Advertising ID) का उपयोग नहीं करते, और हम आपका डेटा कभी नहीं बेचते। आप ऐप में कभी भी अपना अकाउंट और डेटा स्थायी रूप से डिलीट कर सकते हैं।

What we do not do

  • We show no ads and do not use the advertising ID.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data.
  • We do not track your location in the background, and we do not read your contacts or SMS.

Information we collect

  • Sign-in information: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile photo and a Firebase user ID. You can browse as a guest without signing in.
  • Account and profile information: your chosen role (homeowner, service provider or shop owner) and any profile details you enter, such as business name, phone number, area or address, services, experience and rates.
  • Content you add: reviews, ratings, likes, reports about content, and profile, cover, portfolio and shop images you upload.
  • Invoice information: providers and shop owners may enter customer names and contact details, items or services, rates, discounts, totals and notes to create invoices.
  • Location (optional): if you allow location access, the app uses your device location to find your area and pincode so it can show nearby experts and shops. You can type a pincode instead. The app does not collect location in the background.
  • Microphone (optional): used only while you use voice search, through your phone's speech recognition. We do not store voice recordings.
  • Notifications: a push notification token, so we can send you messages such as approval updates and announcements. You can turn notifications off in your phone settings.
  • Usage counts: we count how many times call and WhatsApp buttons are tapped across the app. These counts are totals only and do not record who called whom.
  • Technical information: limited app, device, usage and crash information through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. We do not intentionally send review text, email addresses, phone numbers, names or exact addresses to Analytics.

How we use information

We use information to sign you in and manage your account; show nearby experts and shops; run provider and shop onboarding and check profiles and reviews before they are published; show public profiles, ratings and reviews; store your uploads; provide private invoice history and PDFs; send in-app and push notifications; keep the service safe; and diagnose crashes and improve the app.

Public and private information

Approved provider and shop profiles (including the business phone number and area they choose to show), approved reviews, ratings and approved public images are visible to other users. Pending or rejected profiles and reviews are not shown publicly.

Invoices, customer details in invoices and private account records are never public. Only the provider or shop owner who created them can see them.

Services we use and sharing

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Analytics, Crashlytics and Cloud Messaging) and Google Play services. To turn a location into an area name, the app looks up the pincode or area with the public India Post pincode service and OpenStreetMap. These providers process information on our behalf or under their own terms and privacy policies.

When you call or WhatsApp an expert or shop, that happens in your phone's dialler or in WhatsApp, under their own privacy terms. If a provider shares an invoice PDF through WhatsApp or another app, that sharing is controlled by them.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users and our service.

Security and retention

We protect profiles, uploads, invoices and other private records with Firebase security rules and signed-in access controls. No system is completely risk-free, but we work to keep your information safe.

We keep information while your account is active and as needed to run GharBanoo. When you delete your account, we delete your data as described below, except for limited information we must keep for legal or security reasons.

Deleting your account and data

You can permanently delete your account inside the signed-in GharBanoo app. Step-by-step instructions, and how to ask us by email if you can't use the app, are on gharbanoo.com/delete-account.

Deletion removes your sign-in account, account record, notifications, likes, reviews and reports you made, private invoices, and the profile, portfolio and shop images stored with us. For providers and shop owners, it also removes the public listing and its reviews and likes. Invoice PDFs already saved on a phone or shared through other apps cannot be removed by us.

Your rights

You can view and edit most of your information in the app. You may also contact us to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, or to withdraw consent. We will respond within a reasonable time and as required by applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Children

GharBanoo is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our app or legal duties change. We will post the new version here with a new "Last updated" date, and tell you in the app about important changes.
